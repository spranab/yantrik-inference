# Ternary Bonsai 2 27B on our card and our task

2026-09-19. One RTX 3090 Ti, capped at 75 °C. PrismML's llama.cpp fork,
release `prism-b10709-9a9394a`, Windows CUDA 12.4 build.

Bonsai 2 27B is a ternary copy of Qwen3.8-27B, the model this repository runs.
Everything below compares it against our Q4_K_M build of the same model, on the
same card, through the same binary.

## Speed and size

Measured with `llama-bench`, cooling the card below 50 °C before every run and
alternating the three models over two rounds (`bench_bonsai.sh`). The rounds
agree to within 1%. An earlier Q4_K_M run on a card already at 76 °C came out
20% slower and was discarded as throttled.

| build | size | prefill, 2048 tokens | generation | vs Q4_K_M |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q4_K_M | 15.32 GiB | 925 tok/s | 26.6 tok/s | |
| Bonsai PQ2_0 | 6.70 GiB | 1028 tok/s | 50.9 tok/s | 2.3x smaller, prefill 1.11x, generation 1.91x |
| Bonsai PTQ1_0 | 5.53 GiB | 738 tok/s | 52.7 tok/s | 2.8x smaller, prefill 0.80x, generation 1.98x |

Generation, which is bandwidth-bound, nearly doubles. Prefill, which is
compute-bound, barely moves, and the densest packing makes it slower because
unpacking trits costs arithmetic. On this Ampere card PQ2_0 is the right choice,
as PrismML's own table predicts for A100.

Resident with a 16k context through llama-server: Q4_K_M about 16.3 GB, Bonsai
PQ2_0 about 8.1 GB.

## Accuracy on typed reading

`probe_bonsai.py` runs the SDF classification task through both models behind
the same llama-server, reproducing `yantrik_inference.reader` exactly: same chat
head and tail, same record guard, same question suffix, each option scored as its
best first-token spelling and softmaxed over the options. Same 150 held-out pages
and URL-derived gold as `probe_sdf.py` and `probe_jev.py`.

| | parent type | full path | pages at 0.97 or above | accuracy there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q4_K_M | 0.933 | 0.892 | 93% | 0.971 |
| Bonsai PQ2_0 | **0.947** | **0.912** | 93% | 0.964 |

Two pages apart on each measure, so a tie. The harness is validated by the Q4_K_M
row: our own library reader scored 0.928 on this model. Both models make the same
dominant error, `documentation` read as `reference`, which Jev makes too.

This matters because PrismML's benchmarks are generative and thinking-mode, where
reasoning can cover for a damaged next-token distribution. Typed reading takes the
answer from one position's logits with no such cover. Ternary weights kept those
logits as good as 4-bit ones.

## What it means for this repository

- **Chat gets about 2x.** Generation is the chat endpoint's bottleneck.
- **Decide gets almost nothing.** It is prefill-bound, and prefill moved 11%.
- **Memory is the real gain.** About 9 GB freed on a 24 GB card: at roughly
  150 MiB per sequence and 136 MiB per 4k of context, that is room for about 60
  more decide sequences, or both endpoints resident with large contexts.
- **It needs the fork.** Stock llama.cpp rejects PQ2_0 and PTQ1_0, and our
  llama-cpp-python is built on stock llama.cpp, so adopting Bonsai means moving
  the backend onto the fork.
